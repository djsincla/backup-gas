BackupGAS settings

To save straight to Google Drive, the extension sends the files to a small Apps Script web app of your own, which writes them into your Drive. Leave this blank to use "Download to this computer" only.

Set up the receiver once, then copy the line its page gives you:
  1. Open script.new, paste in receiver/Code.gs, and save.
  2. Deploy › New deployment › Web app. Who has access: Anyone, or your own organization if that is all your admin allows.
  3. Open the /exec address it gives you, and copy the line shown.
  4. Paste it above and click Save.
